FAQ

The app doesn't show my events.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars and make sure Pawlendar has full access.

I'm not getting notifications.

Open System Settings → Notifications → Pawlendar and allow notifications.

Where is the pet?

Pawlendar lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
