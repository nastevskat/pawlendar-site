Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Pawlendar - Pet & Reminders ("the app") respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Calendar access

With your permission, the app reads events from the Calendar on your Mac to show reminders and notifications for upcoming events. This information is processed only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications on your device to remind you of events. No notification data leaves your device.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and makes no network connections.

Your control

You can revoke Calendar or Notification access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
